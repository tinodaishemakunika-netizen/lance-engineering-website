ABOUT LANCE ENGINEERING

Architectural Aluminium & Structural Glazing Specialists

Headquartered at 22 Nuffield Road in Workington, Harare, Lance Engineering delivers precision-fabricated aluminium systems and high-performance architectural glazing across Zimbabwe.

From custom residential folding doors and frameless shower cubicles to multi-storey commercial curtain walls and acoustic office partitions, our engineering team combines durable powder-coated profiles with toughened safety glass.
